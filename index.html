Disconnect TrustTray in BridgeKey
  1. Open the BridgeKey browser extension.
  2. Find its connected sites or site permissions for this website.

Disconnecting a site does not necessarily lock your wallet, revoke every permission, or cancel pending transactions. BridgeKey's published help does not document exact disconnect controls; contact its support if you cannot find them.
