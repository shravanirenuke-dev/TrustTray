# TrustTray
TrustTray is a blockchain-based food donation platform that connects surplus food donors, NGOs, and volunteers to reduce food waste and support people in need. It uses smart contracts to enable transparent food listing, claiming, and pickup confirmation, ensuring secure and traceable donation records.

# Problem Statement

Large amounts of surplus food are wasted every day, while many people struggle to access nutritious meals. Traditional food donation systems often lack transparency, proper coordination, and reliable tracking of donations and deliveries.

# Proposed Solution

TrustTray provides a digital platform where food donors can connect directly with NGOs and volunteers. By integrating blockchain technology, the platform aims to make donation records more transparent, traceable, and reliable.

# Key Features

- Food Donation: Donors can list surplus food with relevant details.
- Food Claiming: NGOs can claim available food donations.
- Pickup Confirmation: Pickup activities can be recorded and tracked.
- Expiry Checks: Helps prevent claiming expired food.
- Verified Users: Access is restricted to authorized and verified participants.
- Blockchain Integration: Records important donation activities through smart contracts.
- Wallet Integration: Enables users to interact with the blockchain through a compatible wallet.

# Technology Stack

- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- Smart Contracts: Solidity
- Blockchain: MST Blockchain
- Development Environment: Remix IDE
- Wallet Integration: Compatible blockchain wallet
- Blockchain Interaction: Ethers.js (if used in the deployed version)

# How It Works

1. A food donor connects their blockchain wallet.
2. The donor lists available surplus food.
3. An NGO views and claims the available donation.
4. A volunteer coordinates the pickup.
5. Pickup confirmation is recorded.
6. Relevant donation activities can be verified through blockchain transactions.

# Target Users
